Common Cyber Threats

Know your enemy: the attacks you are most likely to meet.

Malware

Software built to damage or spy on a device. Often hidden in cracked apps and unknown downloads.

Ransomware

Locks your files and demands payment. Offline backups are the best defence.

Social Engineering

Tricking people instead of hacking machines: fake calls, fake support, fake prizes.

Data Breach

Stolen databases leak emails and passwords. Reusing passwords makes it worse.

Public Wi-Fi Attacks

Open networks can be watched or cloned. Avoid banking on them.

Identity Theft

Personal details used to open accounts or loans in your name.
