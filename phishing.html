Spot the Phish

Fake messages designed to steal your information.

Red Flags

  • Urgent threats: "Your account will be blocked today!"
  • Sender address that looks slightly wrong.
  • Links that do not match the real website.
  • Requests for OTP, PIN or password.
  • Unexpected attachments.

If You Get One

  1. Do not click or reply.
  2. Check the sender through an official channel.
  3. Report it as phishing.
  4. Delete it.
  5. If you clicked, change your password immediately.
