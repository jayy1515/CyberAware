Everyday Safety Checklist

Small habits, big protection.

Update Everything

Install system and app updates quickly; they fix known holes.

Back Up Data

Keep a copy offline or in the cloud.

Check for HTTPS

Look for the padlock before entering any details.

Limit Sharing

Keep phone numbers and locations off public profiles.

Install Only Trusted Apps

Use official stores and read permissions.

Lock Your Devices

Use a screen lock, and log out of shared computers.
